## 🖥️ Share a single tab in meetings
- The share picker now has an **Almac tab** option (the default), next to **Window** and **Entire screen**. Pick any tab, with a thumbnail of each.
- **Share tab audio**: share a video with its sound. Sharing a tab does not need the Screen Recording permission.
- **Fixed:** the share picker could hide its **Share** button and cover tab names with a black box.

## 🔑 Saving passwords after you log in
- **Fixed:** the autofill dropdown could cover the password field or the Sign in button, so the password never got typed or saved. The dropdown now closes or filters as you type.
- Sign-ins that don't change the URL, or that use a non-button "Log in" element, are now detected. Almac offers to save once the login succeeds; a wrong password is not offered.
